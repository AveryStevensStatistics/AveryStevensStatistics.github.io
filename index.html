01 / Customer churn signals

Built a calibrated gradient-boosted model from behavioral and support data; paired it with SHAP-based explanations so customer teams could see the drivers behind each risk score. A thresholding workflow helped focus outreach where it could make a difference.

02 / Demand forecasting

Developed a hierarchical time-series pipeline blending seasonal baselines with event-aware features. Forecast intervals made uncertainty visible, while a lightweight planning view helped regional operators translate forecasts into inventory decisions.

03 / Search by meaning

Prototyped an embedding-based retrieval system for internal research. I created a relevance set with subject-matter experts, compared chunking and reranking strategies, and documented failure cases to keep answers grounded and useful.
